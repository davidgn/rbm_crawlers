# READ FIRST — Technical observations and decisions, RBM post-pass222 Cameroon

## Classification decisions for Cameroon (Pass 222)

- **Librairie Papeterie Bilingue Audrey (Douala)**: Added to registry and compile datasets as a bilingual bookstore offering both new and used books (`single-store used booksellers`).
- **Librairie Saint Paul (Yaoundé)**: Added to registry and compile datasets as a major historic bookstore in downtown Yaoundé (`single-store bookstore layer`).
- **Horizontal Classifieds & Resale Portals**: `CoinAfrique Cameroon`, `Afribaba Cameroon`, `SecondMain237`, `Camerbiz`, `Camerooncom`, `MiniCameroun`, `iConz`, `Fakobook`, and Facebook Marketplace entries synced and categorized under `annex / classifieds`.
