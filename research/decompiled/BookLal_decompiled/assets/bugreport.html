Report a Bug

If you encounter any bugs or crashes, please let us know so we can promptly fix them. Contact our Support Team using the format below:

Email Format:

When reporting a bug via email, please follow these guidelines:

  • Provide a concise subject line.
  • Indicate whether the bug is a crash or a logical issue.
  • Describe the bug or issue in detail.
  • Include information about your device where the bug occurred, such as:
    • Device Company
    • Device Model
    • Android Version
    • RAM
  • Clearly explain how to reproduce the bug. You can provide a step-by-step process or attach a video recording demonstrating the issue.

Please refrain from leaving negative ratings. Give us some time to address the issue, and we'll work diligently to resolve it.
