# Known Gaps and Integrity Notes — 2026-04-25 vFINAL

## High-priority verification gaps
- Actual export/download mechanics for many dynamic map sources still need testing.
- Some counts came from search snippets or source pages and must be rechecked live before harvesting.
- Several private directory surfaces may contain closed businesses, duplicates, stationery shops, school suppliers, or publisher/distributor offices.
- Social-media-only store traces should be treated as provisional unless corroborated.

## Weak/defer markets highlighted during discovery
- LATAM/Caribbean: Haiti, Nicaragua, El Salvador, Honduras, Puerto Rico, some Eastern Caribbean microstates, Greenland.
- Europe: Andorra, Liechtenstein, Malta, parts of Balkans, small territories.
- MENA: Sudan, Syria, Yemen, Libya, Iraq, Algeria remain status-sensitive or weak.
- Africa: Eritrea, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, São Tomé and Príncipe, Comoros, Seychelles, Libya, Sudan, Palau/Marshall/Tuvalu-like microstates in Pacific analogues.
- Asia: Bhutan, Laos, North Korea, Macau, Mongolia are anchor-focused; China requires provincial reconstruction.
- Oceania/Pacific: Tuvalu, Nauru, Palau, Marshall Islands, CNMI, Niue, Tokelau require manual/anchor treatment.

## Closure/status-sensitive examples
- Antigua & Barbuda: Best of Books reported closed August 11, 2025; keep historical unless reverified live.
- Greenland: reports that the last bookstore closed; verify current status before live registry claims.
- Hong Kong / China / North Korea: political and continuity sensitivity; preserve last-seen and avoid stale independent-store assumptions.
- Conflict-affected countries: Syria, Sudan, Yemen, Iraq, Palestine/Gaza, South Sudan, Somalia/Somaliland, Myanmar.

## Data-model cautions
- Chain branches and independent stores can coexist, but source_class must indicate branch locator vs independent registry.
- Publisher-sales-point lists are highly useful but can include distributors, schoolbook depots, and wholesalers.
- Church and campus bookstore networks are valid book-retail infrastructure but must be typed separately.
- Used/antiquarian sources are valuable overlays, not substitutes for general bookstore registries.
